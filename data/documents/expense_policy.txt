Company Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reimbursement for approved business expenses.

Domestic travel expenses require manager approval.

International travel requires manager and department-head approval.

Hotel reimbursement is limited to the approved accommodation allowance.

Meals during business travel may be reimbursed up to the applicable daily limit.

Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

Original receipts or valid digital receipts must be retained for reimbursement.
